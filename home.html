About Us

Murliwala Sarees draws its inspiration from the timeless traditions and elegance associated with Shri Krishna(Murliwala), reflecting these values in every saree and lehenga we offer.

We believe that everything happens for a reason, and that the right customers are naturally drawn to us. Our reputation for quality and service speaks for itself, and we trust in the loyalty and satisfaction of those who choose to visit us.

Today, our showroom is renowned within a 100 km radius of our location. Customers go out of their way to visit us, drawn by our unmatched quality and service. Even our carry bags reflect simplicity—featuring just "Murliwala" and a phone number, without an address or owner’s name. Established in 2010 in a small room, Murliwala Sarees has grown into a symbol of trust and tradition. Owned by Mrs. Arti Gupta and Mr. Vijay Kumar, we take pride in creating timeless designs for every occasion.

Our ambition is to provide every customer with unmatched quality, style, and service, ensuring a memorable shopping experience.
