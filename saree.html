Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to drape a saree?

To drape a saree, start by tucking one end into your petticoat, wrap it around your waist, and pleat the fabric before securing it with a pin at the waist and shoulder.

How do I care for my silk saree?

For silk sarees, dry clean only, avoid exposing them to direct sunlight, and store them in a cotton bag to prevent damage.

What type of saree is suitable for a wedding?

For weddings, traditional sarees like Banarasi, Kanjivaram, or georgette sarees with intricate designs and heavy work are popular choices.

How can I choose the right saree for my body type?

For petite figures, go for lighter fabrics like chiffon or georgette. Curvier body types may opt for sarees that drape well, such as silk or crepe.
