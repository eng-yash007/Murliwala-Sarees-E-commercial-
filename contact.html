About Us

Murliwala Sarees is inspired by Shri Krishna (Murliwala), reflecting elegance and tradition in every saree and lehenga we offer. We believe in letting our work speak for itself, as we have never relied on advertisements. Our philosophy is simple: "Jo hota hai, sab upar wala karta hai. Customers bhi wahi bhejte hain, khud bankar aate hain." Today, our showroom is renowned within a 100 km radius of our location. Customers go out of their way to visit us, drawn by our unmatched quality and service. Even our carry bags reflect simplicity—featuring just "Murliwala" and a phone number, without an address or owner’s name. Established in 2010 in a small room, Murliwala Sarees has grown into a symbol of trust and tradition. Owned by Mrs. Arti Gupta and Mr. Vijay Kumar, we take pride in creating timeless designs for every occasion.

Our ambition is to provide every customer with unmatched quality, style, and service, ensuring a memorable shopping experience.
